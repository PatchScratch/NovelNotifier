A clean-room, open-source replacement for the abandoned "Notifications for
Novel Updates" app.

NovelNotifier shows your Novel Updates reading lists with covers and unread
counts, notifies you when new chapters release (grouped or individual
alerts, surviving reboots), and marks chapters read in both directions —
syncing your position across devices via your NU feeds.

• Browse your library as a grid or list with search, sort modes and unread badges
• Site search with live suggestions and full paged results
• Discover rankings (popular, weekly, monthly…)
• Series pages: INFO + full RELEASES table, list changes written back to the site
• Built-in reader: chapters as clean styled text (sepia/dark/light, serif/sans,
  adjustable size) with the full web page one tap away — including decoding of
  font-substitution anti-scraping hosts
• Themes: light/dark/AMOLED black/Material You, or bring your own via a JSON
  theme file
• One-zip backups via the system file picker (Google Drive, USB, anywhere) —
  never containing your credentials
• Paced, polite background sync that respects the site (no Cloudflare-bypass
  tricks — real challenges are shown for you to complete)

Requires a free Novel Updates account, used only to read your own lists and
write your own marks back. No ads, no analytics, no trackers.
