NovelNotifier 3.1 — paged epub reading and watched folders.

PAGED READING (your own ePubs)
• A third reader mode for local-library books: ⋮ → Paged view (epub) renders the book through the Readium navigator as real horizontal pages — swipe to turn, your page theme and serif/sans applied, ‹ Prev / Next › still move chapters. Scroll text view remains for translation work.

WATCHED FOLDERS (Calibre workflow)
• Linked folders are now watched: every time you open the library, new ePubs dropped into a linked folder (Calibre uploads, downloads — anything) are imported automatically, with a 5-minute cooldown per sweep.
• ☰ → Re-scan linked folders forces a sweep immediately.
• Moved or reorganized your files? The old dead entries surface under the 🧹 missing-files row for one-tap cleanup, and the new locations import on the next scan.

ALSO
• Duplicate detection hardened across the board (comma-named re-rips, volume-in-filename matching).
