Major revision — NovelNotifier is now a three-site webnovel app. Japanese sites join Novel Updates as first-class workspaces, each with its own drawer and settings: Syosetu (なろう) via its official JSON API — rankings, advanced search, in-app login, bookmark library sync, and the R18 sister sites (Nocturne/Moonlight/Ladies) behind an 18+ gate with per-site toggles — and Kakuyomu (カクヨム) via the site's open GraphQL endpoint — rankings across 12 genres, paged search, full ordered table of contents, R18 browsing behind an 18+ gate, and device-local bookmarks that ride backups. All sites share the in-app reader and the offline ePub library (volume/100-chapter split, bulk download that survives screen-off, per-series translation-group filter on NU). Full English/Japanese app-language toggle: display follows your choice, while anything stored or sent to a site stays in the site's native terms. Also new: the reader decodes anti-scrape font-substitution hosts, and a Japanese README ships alongside the English one.
